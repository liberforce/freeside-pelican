Every now and then, I see someone linking to a web page using  the "here" or
"click here" link style. This is quite annoying, and also unhelpful

- for indexation robots used by the web search engines
- for humans who can't figure out without reading the context what is the
  information you want to share
- for humans with disabilities

meant: Just read [why linking with "click here" is bad
practice](http://www.cs.tut.fi/%7Ejkorpela/www/click.html).
