Yesterday night, after a long walk to find a restaurant open on a Sunday, and two of the nice places found on the internet turning out to be closed (as in "the restaurant doesn't even exist anymore"), we eventually found the <a href="http://www.culuca.com" hreflang="es">Culuca</a>.

On the eleven people of our group, seems everyone was satisfied with the food quality. We had been looking for a while to a place where we could eat some <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Octopus" hreflang="en">polpo</a>, and had mostly given up when we found this restaurant/bar. Luckily, they also serve some great *polpo a la plancha* (12,90€), other meals being really good too, and less expensive. With the wine (15€ for a bottle of galician red wine), and dessert (cheese cake around 3.50€, <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petit_G%C3%A2teau" hreflang="en">petit gâteau</a> at 4.50€), the bill you should expect is around 19€ per person.
