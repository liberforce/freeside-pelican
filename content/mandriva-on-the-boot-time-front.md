It's nice to see that [Mandriva is working on shortening the boot time](http://blog.crozat.net/2008/09/improving-boot-time-on-general-linux.html){hreflang="en"}. Even if we're not at a 5 seconds boot (which, I agree with fcrozat, is unreal - what people should ask for is flawless resume instead), I've seen on [some of his](http://people.mandriva.com/%7Efcrozat/bootchart/) [bootchart](http://www.bootchart.org/) graphs a 10% speedup which is cool. As it's been a long time since I didn't use bootchart, and tried it again and benchmarked [my Mandriva 2008.1/GNOME system](http://hardware4linux.info/system/3032/){hreflang="en"}, in case it's useful for someone.
